Appendix C

Sources

Primary sources for each pattern and rule in the book, grouped by topic. Lantern Pet Supply, its people, and its data are made up. Wage rates in the examples are illustrative.

C.1 Foundations

  • W. H. Inmon, Building the Data Warehouse, first edition, QED Technical Publishing Group, 1992. Later editions by Wiley.
  • C. J. Date, Hugh Darwen, and Nikos Lorentzos, Temporal Data and the Relational Model, Morgan Kaufmann, 2003. The source of sixth normal form.
  • Richard T. Snodgrass, Developing Time-Oriented Database Applications in SQL, Morgan Kaufmann, 2000. The classic reference on bitemporal data.

C.2 Dimensional modeling

  • Ralph Kimball and Margy Ross, The Data Warehouse Toolkit, third edition, Wiley, 2013. First edition by Ralph Kimball, 1996.
  • Kimball Group, Dimensional modeling techniques, including slowly changing dimensions, bridges, and the bus matrix.

C.3 Data Vault

  • Dan Linstedt, Data Vault Series 1: Data Vault Overview, TDAN.com, July 2002.
  • Dan Linstedt and Michael Olschimke, Building a Scalable Data Warehouse with Data Vault 2.0, Morgan Kaufmann, 2015.
  • Data Vault Alliance, the method's home, including Data Vault 2.1 material.

C.4 Anchor modeling

  • Olle Regardt, Lars Rönnbäck, Maria Bergholtz, Paul Johannesson, and Petia Wohed, Anchor Modeling: An Agile Modeling Technique using the Sixth Normal Form for Structurally and Temporally Evolving Data, ER 2009, Springer. Best paper award.
  • The same authors, Anchor Modeling: Agile Information Modeling in Evolving Data Environments, Data and Knowledge Engineering, volume 69, issue 12, 2010.
  • Lars Rönnbäck, Posits and Assertions, 2015. Formal definitions for the concurrent-reliance-temporal model.
  • Anchor modeling site and the authors' open-source generator and documentation.

C.5 Activity schema and unified star schema

  • Ahmed Elsamadisi, Activity Schema specification, version 2.0, December 2022.
  • Bill Inmon and Francesco Puppini, The Unified Star Schema: An Agile and Resilient Approach to Data Warehouse and Analytics Design, Technics Publications, 2020.

C.6 Laws and rules in the case study

  • California Labor Commissioner, Overtime FAQ and Meal periods FAQ.
  • US Department of Labor, Fact Sheet 21: Recordkeeping requirements under the FLSA.
  • California Attorney General, California Consumer Privacy Act.
  • PCI Security Standards Council, PCI Data Security Standard.
  • US Food and Drug Administration, Animal and veterinary recalls and withdrawals.
