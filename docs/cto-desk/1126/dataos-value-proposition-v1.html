DataOS value proposition

By persona · 18 November 2026

This draft names the people who decide whether DataOS spreads in a company. For each one, it says what they are measured on, what hurts, what DataOS gives them, and what proof they need.

It uses the people from How one company adopts DataOS, and one of the entry products from "DataOS for Engineering Leaders", section 5.

The value in five lines
  • The engineer: give your agent company data in minutes, with each person's own access, and ship it without a security fight.
  • The engineering leader: say yes to agents from any tool, with one standard for what they can reach.
  • The CISO: every agent acts as the person who uses it, and every action is on record.
  • The data leader: define a number once, and every agent uses it.
  • The business colleague: use the agents that your team needs, with your own access, from day one.

Terms used below

AgentAn AI program that does a task for a person.
GatewayOne governed route between agents and company systems. Every call passes through it.
Data productCompany data packaged with its definitions, owner and quality checks.
MCPThe open standard that connects AI agents to tools and data.
Audit logThe record of who used which agent, and what data the agent read or changed.
Free tierA developer uses DataOS for free, on real company data, for themselves only, with a limit on agents and workloads.
The wallThe point where an agent reaches company systems for other people. Payment starts there.
ViewerA person who only uses agents. Viewers are free.

1The entry product behind the value

Section 5 of the paper lists five candidates for the first entry product. This page assumes one of them: governance of agents built anywhere, delivered as a gateway. Research decides the final choice. If another candidate wins, the value for the engineer changes, and the value for the other personas stays.

With this entry product, DataOS starts governed and becomes meaningful. On the first day, it is the gateway between any agent and the company's systems. Each call runs with the user's own permissions, and each call goes into one audit log. This needs no data products.

Over time, DataOS sees which data the agents use, and where two agents calculate the same number in different ways. It then proposes data products from that use, and the data team approves them. Business meaning grows where it matters. The longer a company runs agents through DataOS, the more of its business meaning lives there.

Running agents on DataOS remains available, but it is the second step. The value below does not depend on it.

3The five personas

Each persona uses the same fields, so the value is easy to compare.

The engineerFirst user

Priya in the journey. She works on the platform team of a mid-size company. The team runs the cloud, the integrations and the data warehouse.

Measured onDelivery speed, uptime, requests closed and no security incidents.
Job nowShip the agents that colleagues ask for, and keep them safe.
Pains, in her words"Every system needs its own sign-in and its own connector." "I cannot share this without a review that takes weeks." "I get paged for apps that I never approved." "My team blocks the apps that business teams build, and the queue keeps growing." "The agent got the margin wrong."
What she builds todayA sign-in flow and a connector for each system. A map of who can see what. An audit log, masking for personal data, and limits on queries. Definitions for business numbers. None of this is her agent.
What DataOS givesOne connection from her agent to company systems. Each call uses the user's own permissions. Ready tools for each source, one audit log, traces for each answer, masking and query limits. Business definitions follow as data products grow. Her team stops being the bottleneck. It runs the platform, and business teams build on it.
First hourShe signs up with her work email. She adds one DataOS address to the agent tool that she already uses. She connects one real source with her own login. Within the hour, her agent answers a real question with her permissions, and a trace shows each record that it read.
Proof she needsIt works with her agent tool. It gives a governed answer on real data in an hour. The security team accepts the audit log.
Objection"I can build this myself."
Our answerShe can build the first connector. Six connectors with per-person access, audit and masking, kept up to date, are a product. DataOS is free to start, and she owns her agent.

Give your agent company data in minutes, with each person's own access, and ship it without a security fight.

The engineering leaderBuyer

Rosa in the journey. The CIO, the CTO or the chief architect. At smaller companies, the VP of engineering. She decides whether DataOS becomes the company standard.

Measured onHow much of the company uses AI safely, the cost of AI, and risk.
Job nowLet the whole company build with AI, and keep it safe and affordable.
Pains, in her words"Nobody can list our agents." "A customer asked who accessed their data, and I could not answer." "My team is the team that says no." "We pay for AI tools on credit cards."
What DataOS givesOne standard route from every agent to company data, whichever tool built the agent. A list of agents, with who built them, who uses them and what they reach. Per-person access and one audit log across systems. One set of business numbers. No lock-in to one cloud or one model.
First experienceA usage report from her platform team: which agents run through DataOS, who uses them and what data they reach.
Proof she needsA reference from a similar company. One security review of the platform, not one per agent. It works with Microsoft, Databricks or Snowflake, which she already owns.
Objection"Microsoft or Databricks already governs our agents."
Our answerEach one governs the agents and data inside its own platform. Her company runs several. DataOS covers what crosses them, and works with each one.

Say yes to agents from any tool, with one standard for what they can reach.

The CISOCan block the deal

The chief information security officer. Can stop any product that touches customer or employee data.

Measured onIncidents, audit findings and compliance.
Job nowKeep data safe while agents spread through the company.
Pains, in their words"Agents run on shared service accounts." "I cannot show who accessed what." "Every new agent is a new review."
What DataOS givesEvery agent acts as the person who uses it. One audit log across systems, with export. Approval before go-live for sensitive data. Policies set once and applied to every agent. One review of the platform.
First experienceThe security pack, our set of documents for a security review, and one review of the platform.
Proof they needAn audit export that answers a real customer questionnaire. The security certifications that the product team confirms.
Objection"A vendor that touches all our systems is a new risk."
Our answerDataOS replaces many direct connections with one route that is on record. It uses each user's own permissions. The product team must state what data DataOS stores.

Every agent acts as the person who uses it, and every action is on record.

Appendix A: Assumptions and open questions

The page assumes these points. Each one needs a decision, or a fact from the product team.

  • Free tier: the plan is real data for the developer alone, with a limit on agents and workloads. Sharing is the first payment. Sample data only for a company whose security team blocks a free hosted service.
  • Per-person access depends on each source system. Salesforce, SharePoint and most warehouses support sign-in on behalf of the user. Older ERP systems often do not.
  • What data DataOS stores on the gateway path, and for how long.
  • Which security certifications DataOS holds today.
  • How DataOS proposes data products from use, and how much of that AI can draft.
  • Prices for builders, and whether a price per builder slows adoption.
