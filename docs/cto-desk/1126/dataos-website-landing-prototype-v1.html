Questions

Common questions

Is DataOS new?

No. DataOS runs in production at large enterprises as a platform for data products. Building, running and governing agents is the new part, and we build it with design partners.

Do we need to move our data?

No. DataOS works on your existing warehouse, lakehouse or catalog. Agents reach the data through governed data products.

Which agent frameworks work with DataOS?

Any client that supports MCP can use DataOS data products today. MCP is an open standard that connects agents to tools and data.

What does a design partner do?

You pick one agent that a team in your company needs. Your engineers build it with ours, and you tell us what to change. Design partners get early access and a say in the product.

Who is behind DataOS?

DataOS is built by Modern, the company that has run DataOS for enterprise customers since before the agent era.
