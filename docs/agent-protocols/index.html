Agent protocols: field guides

Three open protocols now decide how agents reach tools, each other and the people using them. Each guide covers one protocol end to end: how it works, how it evolved, who has adopted it, and what it does and doesn't protect.

Every claim is sourced, with vendor and self-reported figures marked. They are snapshots as of November 2026, and the protocols move fast.
